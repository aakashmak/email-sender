## Research Guidelines - Data & AI Role Focus

Research to identify data, analytics, and AI initiatives and quantifiable pain points.

### 1. **Identify Their Role + Primary Initiative** (CRITICAL)
   - What's their exact title? (Data Science Manager, Head of Analytics, VP Data, Director of BI, Chief Data Officer)
   - What initiative/system are they building or responsible for?
   - Look for keywords: data platform, ML pipeline, analytics, forecasting, AI, reporting, data quality, insights

### 2. **Company Data & Analytics Challenges** (Pick 1-2 most relevant to their role)
   Search for evidence of these specific concerns:

   **Data Scientist / ML Engineer:**
   - **Model development**: Building, training, deploying ML/AI models for prediction or classification
   - **LLM / Agentic AI**: Integrating LLMs, RAG pipelines, vector search, or multi-agent systems
   - **NLP / document intelligence**: Text extraction, document processing, sentiment analysis
   - **Data quality for ML**: Training data pipelines, feature engineering, data validation
   - **Model monitoring**: Drift detection, retraining pipelines, performance tracking

   **Data Engineer / Analytics Engineer:**
   - **Pipeline reliability**: ETL/ELT failures, data freshness, ingestion bottlenecks
   - **Data warehouse optimization**: Snowflake/BigQuery/Redshift query costs, performance tuning
   - **Real-time data**: Streaming pipelines, event-driven architectures, IoT data ingestion
   - **Data governance**: Schema management, data lineage, access controls, compliance
   - **Cloud data platform**: Azure/AWS/GCP data stack modernization or migration

   **Business Analyst / Product Analyst:**
   - **Self-serve analytics**: Enabling non-technical teams to access and explore data
   - **KPI dashboards**: Executive reporting, operational dashboards in Power BI or Tableau
   - **Forecasting & planning**: Demand forecasting, financial projections, capacity planning
   - **A/B testing & experimentation**: Product analytics, funnel analysis, conversion optimization
   - **Data accuracy**: Manual reporting errors, reconciliation issues, single source of truth

   **AI / ML Engineering:**
   - **LLM / agent pipelines**: Orchestration, multi-model routing, retries, cost control
   - **RAG / knowledge systems**: Retrieval accuracy, chunking, embedding pipelines
   - **Model serving**: Inference latency, throughput, batching, GPU cost
   - **Evals and quality**: Measuring output quality, regression testing for prompts
   - **Data pipelines for AI**: ETL into vector stores, fine-tuning data prep

### 3. **Recent Job Postings** (CRITICAL - determines email mode)

   **First: check the contact's title.**
   - If the contact is an individual contributor (Data Scientist, Data Analyst, ML Engineer, Analytics Engineer, BI Developer, etc.) → **always use MODE B**, regardless of job postings. ICs can't hire you; peer-to-peer is the only angle that works.
   - If the contact is a hiring decision-maker (Data Science Manager, Head of Data, VP Analytics, Director of BI, CDO, CTO, Recruiter, etc.) → check for job postings to decide Mode A vs B.

   For decision-makers only:
   - Search for job openings at their company posted in the last 30 days
   - Look for: Data Scientist, Data Analyst, ML Engineer, Analytics Engineer, Data Engineer, Business Analyst, Product Analyst, BI Developer
   - If found: note the exact role title, team, and specific requirements from the JD — the email will reference this directly
   - If not found: note the absence — the email will use the insight-led approach instead
   - Search LinkedIn Jobs, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, and company careers page

### 4. **LinkedIn Activity**
   - Search for their recent LinkedIn posts (last 7-14 days)
   - Look for posts about: hiring, scaling challenges, infrastructure work, team growth
   - Technical topics they're discussing or sharing

### 5. **Company Tech Stack & Scale Indicators**
   - What data tools do they use? (Snowflake, Databricks, dbt, Spark, Airflow, Power BI, Tableau, Looker, Azure, AWS, BigQuery)
   - Scale indicators: data volume, number of pipelines, team size, records processed
   - Recent data/analytics blog posts, conference talks, or case studies

### 6. **Recent Company News**
   - Series B/C/D funding (indicates growth/scaling challenges)
   - Product launches (new infrastructure needs)
   - Geographic expansion (reliability/compliance needs)

### Output Format:
Summarize in 4-5 bullets:
- **EMAIL MODE**: MODE A (job posting found: [exact role title]) or MODE B (no posting found)
- Primary role + initiative they own
- 1-2 B+ engineering challenges (with evidence)
- Tech stack or scale indicators
- Key insight or observation to use as the hook (for Mode B)
